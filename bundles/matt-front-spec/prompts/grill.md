# Interview me to shape a spec

You are about to write a specification for a feature I want to build, but first
you must understand it. Interview me before you write anything.

Read the grilling skill you are pointed to and follow it. Ask one sharp question
at a time, wait for my answer, and dig into the vague or hand-wavy parts. Do not
write any files during this conversation — this is the interview, not the spec.

When you believe you understand the feature well enough to specify it, say so
plainly and stop asking questions. A later step, after I approve, will ask you to
write the spec from everything we discussed.
